One person. One Clubvel account. Different roles in different groups.

What Clubvel helps you manage

Clear group records without inventing financial scores, balances or payment states that are not supported by recorded activity.

Contributions

See recorded contribution information for each group and period, including confirmed and outstanding states where the group has recorded them.

Payment proofs

Upload proof against the relevant group contribution so the record stays connected to the payment it belongs to.

Groups & roles

Create a group or accept an invitation. Your role belongs to that group, not to your whole Clubvel account.

Claims & payouts

See claim and payout information only when it has actually been recorded for you and your group.

Invitations

Invited members choose whether to accept. Membership is created only after acceptance.

Your history

Clubvel builds a record from verifiable activity in your groups rather than displaying a made-up Trust Score.

Simple by design

1

Create your account

Register as a person. You do not choose an account-wide admin, treasurer or member role.

2

Create or join a group

Create your own group or accept an invitation to a group you know.

3

Keep the group record current

Record contributions, proofs and group activity so members can see what has actually happened.
